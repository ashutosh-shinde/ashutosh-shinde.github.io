The theory, in short

  • Food and eaters. The food is renewed at rate I and eaten; consumers grow in proportion to what they eat (γ) and die at rate δ.
  • Holling type II uptake βR/(R0 + R): little food → eating is limited by finding it; plenty → by handling it, up to β.
  • R*. The food level where a consumer just replaces itself: R* = δR0/(γβ − δ) (needs γβ > δ).
  • Alone, species i settles at R = Ri* with Ci* = Iγi/δi.
  • Together, the species with the lower R* wins: it pulls the food down to its own R*, where the other's growth rate is negative (its invasion rate γβR*/(R0 + R*) − δ < 0).
  • Competitive exclusion. Two consumers fully dependent on one resource cannot coexist unless R1* = R2* exactly (a fragile tie).
  • Not the biggest eater. The winner is the one that survives when food is scarcest, not the one that eats most when it is plenty.
  • Fixed points (correcting a sign in the README draft): at equilibrium R*(β1C1/(R01 + R*) + β2C2/(R02 + R*)) = I.
