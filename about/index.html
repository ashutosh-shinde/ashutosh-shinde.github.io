About me

Ashutosh Shinde

I studied mathematics at NISER, then spent an amazing year conserving, capturing and tracking wildlife with RESQ CT. Now I am working on mathematical models of ecosystems.

Research interests

  • Mathematical ecology and spatial ecosystem dynamics
  • Integration of field data (GIS, radio telemetry) with ecological theory
  • Human–wildlife conflict and its mitigation

Every line, in order

    In the field

    A year as a wildlife field technician with RESQ Charitable Trust, Pune, 2025 – 2026.

    • DeerSeveral deer species captured and moved to Sahyadri Tiger Reserve to build up its prey base, and to the wildlife sanctuary at Ambajogai to re-establish their populations.
    • CrocodilesCrocodiles translocated to Melghat Tiger Reserve.
    • TigersThree dispersing tigers tracked across three states by radio, thermal drone and on foot.
    • LeopardsLeopards monitored, captured, rescued and released across the human-dominated landscapes of Pune and Junnar.
    • Living with wildlifeHuman–wildlife conflict mitigation alongside the Forest Department, and awareness sessions for the people who share their land with wildlife.
    • RescuesBirds and small mammals such as civets rescued across the city, and technical rescues in fast-changing situations.
    • The peopleCountless memories with the most remarkable and inventive colleagues and mentors I have known. Thank you, RESQ.
