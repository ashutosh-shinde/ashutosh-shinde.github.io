About me

Ashutosh Shinde

I trained in abstract mathematics at NISER, then traded the blackboard for the field: a year with RESQ CT, rescuing, capturing and tracking wildlife. Now I bring the two together, building mathematical models of ecosystems.

Research interests

  • Mathematical ecology and spatial ecosystem dynamics
  • Early warning signals and critical transitions
  • Collective behaviour and emergence
  • Integration of field data (GIS, radio telemetry) with ecological theory
  • Human–wildlife conflict and its mitigation

Every line, in order

    In the field

    A year as a wildlife field technician with RESQ Charitable Trust, Pune, 2025 – 2026.

    Every day in the field brought a new challenge, yet the long days never felt hard alongside the craziest, most inventive and most fun colleagues and mentors I have ever known. Thank you for countless memories.

    • DeerSeveral deer species captured and moved to Sahyadri Tiger Reserve to build up its prey base, and to the wildlife sanctuary at Ambajogai to re-establish their populations.
    • CrocodilesCrocodiles translocated to Melghat Tiger Reserve, along with many rescues and releases.
    • TigersThree dispersing tigers tracked across three states by radio, thermal drone and on foot.
    • LeopardsLeopards monitored, captured, rescued and released across the human-dominated landscapes of Pune and Junnar.
    • CoexistenceHuman–wildlife conflict mitigation alongside the Forest Department, and awareness sessions for communities that share their spaces with wildlife.
    • RescuesRescues across the city, from birds to civets, jackals and rusty-spotted cats, and technical rope-access rescues from cliffs, wells and dams.
